## 02-closed-stall

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Generate a new standalone landscape 16:9 cinematic film reference frame, scene 2 of a StallSeeker Malaysian food-stall promotional video. Input image is a character and visual-style reference, not an edit target. Preserve the exact young Malaysian male student's face, short black hair, medium tan skin, plain cream T-shirt and dark navy backpack from the reference. Show him in three-quarter side profile, standing beside a small closed roadside Malaysian food kiosk, looking at its closed counter with quiet disappointment. A rolled-down unbranded metal shutter, bare empty serving shelf and folded parasol clearly communicate that the stall is closed. He holds his black phone lowered at waist height. No shop signs or lettering, no legible phone UI. Warm late-afternoon light, authentic simple Malaysian neighbourhood, orange awning echoing reference, realistic skin and hands, medium-wide eye-level 35mm shot. Keep the stall itself visibly unoccupied, no food ready for sale. No captions, watermark, collage, dystopian decay or exaggerated expressions. Leave lower region suitable for captions added in editing.

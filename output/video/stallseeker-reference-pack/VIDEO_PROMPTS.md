# Google Flow prompts — free-credit workflow

Use these prompts with the supplied PNG images as start frames. This pack contains images, scripts and prompts; it does not contain generated video clips.

## Settings

1. Open a Flow project. In the prompt box select Video, then Frames, and add the corresponding image as the start frame. Labels can vary with interface updates.
2. Choose landscape 16:9, one output, and an 8-second generation if your selected model supports it.
3. Check the model and displayed credit cost before each generation. Use a lower-cost model offered by your account.
4. Generate the four human scenes first. The logo and phone reference can be used as still images in an editor, so they do not need Flow generations.
5. Add one continuous voice-over and captions during editing. These prompts deliberately avoid on-camera speech, so lip synchronisation is unnecessary.

Google's published free plan and credit page checked on 2 November 2026 list 50 daily Flow credits. At the published Gemini Omni Flash 720p price of 12 credits for an 8-second generation, four successful single-output generations total 48 credits before any retries. If a different model is selected, the cost changes. Check the amount shown in Flow; balances, models and availability may differ or change. Non-subscribers can have generation restrictions during peak demand. No successful-generation or same-day completion guarantee is implied.

## Clip 1 — Searching for a stall

Start frame: `01-search.png`

Prompt:

> Animate this image into an 8-second photorealistic cinematic shot. Preserve the young man's face, cream T-shirt, navy backpack, black smartphone and Malaysian food-stall setting. He briefly glances down at his phone, then looks slowly along the stalls with a mildly uncertain expression. Use a very gentle camera push-in. Natural background pedestrian movement, subtle cooking steam and warm late-afternoon light. Keep his body movement small and believable. The phone screen stays turned away. Preserve clothing and stall geometry throughout. Soft street ambience only, no dialogue, narration, music, subtitles, graphics or new logos.

## Clip 2 — The closed stall

Start frame: `02-closed-stall.png`

Prompt:

> Animate this image into an 8-second photorealistic cinematic shot. Keep the same student's face, cream shirt, navy backpack and black phone unchanged. He looks at the closed stall counter, pauses, and gently lowers his shoulders in disappointment. Slow subtle lateral camera movement. The metal shutter remains closed and the stall stays empty for the entire shot. Retain warm natural afternoon light and realistic proportions. No shutter opening, food appearing, new people approaching him or dramatic acting. Quiet neighbourhood ambience only, no speech, narration, music, captions or generated lettering.

## Clip 4 — Customer meets vendor

Start frame: `06-food-handoff.png`

Prompt:

> Animate this image into an 8-second photorealistic cinematic shot. Preserve both people's faces, the student's cream shirt and navy backpack, the vendor's charcoal shirt and burnt-orange apron, and the Malaysian stall. Begin from the existing handoff pose. The vendor gently releases the single kraft takeaway bag into the student's hands. The student draws it slightly toward himself; both exchange a natural small smile. Use very restrained movement and a slow gentle camera pull-back. The bag remains one solid object with a consistent folded top, without handles, and no duplicated hands or changing faces. Warm late-afternoon light and quiet stall ambience. No payment action, delivery rider, new props, spoken dialogue, narration, music, captions or graphics.

## Logo and actual app scenes — no Flow generation required

- `03-stallseeker-logo.png`: use the original logo in the editor. Add title and tagline as editable text. Gentle fade or scale is sufficient.
- `04-phone-insert.png`: optional still background with a neutral phone screen. Place an authentic app screenshot on the display with perspective/corner adjustment if your editor supports it. Full-screen app recordings are simpler and clearer; using the phone mockup is optional.
- Record the actual customer discovery map, stall menu/status, Follow and directions features, and the vendor dashboard/menu updates. Do not ask Flow to invent the interface.

## Assemble the 105-second edit

| Timeline | Material |
| --- | --- |
| 0–15 sec | Clip 1 (8 sec), then a still crop or gentle zoom on reference 1 (7 sec) |
| 15–30 sec | Clip 2 (8 sec), then a still crop or gentle zoom on reference 2 (7 sec) |
| 30–40 sec | Original logo, project title and narration (10 sec) |
| 40–65 sec | Actual customer app recordings (25 sec) |
| 65–85 sec | Clip 3 (8 sec), then actual vendor app recordings (12 sec) |
| 85–105 sec | Clip 4 (8 sec), a held/cropped handoff image (7 sec), then logo closing (5 sec) |

The final edit is 1 minute 45 seconds, with 32 seconds of Flow-generated footage and the remaining time devoted to original app demonstrations and still-image/title animation. This matches the assignment's requirement to use AI assistance in creative production. Use STORYBOARD.md for the complete narration and captions.

If credits run low, use an unanimated reference image with a gentle pan or zoom for that shot, or continue when credits refresh. Export MP4 after adding the narration and captions. Verify the actual final duration remains between 1 and 2 minutes.
